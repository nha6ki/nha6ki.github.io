## Overview

From June 2017 to March 2018, I participated in *Kagayake Aichi Sustainable Laboratory*, a sustainability initiative organized by Aichi Prefecture. The program brought university students and partner organizations together to investigate environmental challenges and propose practical solutions.

I was a member of **Team BTMU**, a four-student team collaborating with the Bank of Tokyo-Mitsubishi UFJ, Ltd. (BTMU).

## Research & Proposal

Our team explored ways to encourage younger generations to adopt environmentally responsible banking services.

We proposed **"Visa Debit Card × Eco Passbook: Toward a Cashless and Paperless Society Starting with Students,"** combining the bank's debit card and electronic passbook services to promote cashless transactions and reduce paper consumption.

Our proposal included:

- **Student Cashback Incentives:** Additional cashback benefits for students using Visa debit cards together with Eco Passbook.
- **Awareness and Promotion:** Social media campaigns to increase awareness and encourage adoption of digital banking services among students.
- **Support for Overseas Use:** Educational initiatives to help students understand and use Visa debit cards while studying or traveling abroad.
- **Simplified Digital Banking:** A future concept for making the transition to electronic passbooks easier through ATM-based procedures.

These initiatives were developed as proposals rather than independently implemented banking services.

## Final Presentation

**Dec 3, 2017 · Aichi Shukutoku University**

Team BTMU presented its research findings and proposed solutions at the program's 2017 final presentation event, **placing third out of 10 participating teams**.
