## Overview

From May 2019 to October 2020, I participated in the Robotic Smart Home (RSH) project through Aichi Prefectural University.

Led by Fujita Health University, RSH explored how assistive robotics and smart-home technologies could support safe, comfortable, and independent living, particularly for older adults and people with disabilities.

My work focused on Toyota's [Human Support Robot (HSR)](https://global.toyota/en/detail/8709541), a compact mobile robot equipped with a robotic arm and gripper for picking up, carrying, and retrieving everyday objects.

## Development & Technologies

I developed and demonstrated applications for robotic object manipulation, voice-controlled assistance, and human–robot interaction using the HSR and Robot Operating System (ROS).

My work included:

- **Voice-Activated Robot Assistance:** Implemented a multi-step task that allowed users to summon the HSR through voice commands and have it pick up and dispose of a plastic bottle.
- **Object Detection & Manipulation:** Worked on object detection, grasp planning, and control of the HSR's robotic arm and gripper to handle everyday objects.
- **Voice-Controlled Home Automation:** Implemented voice commands to open and close window blinds and turn room lights on and off.
- **Hand Gesture Recognition:** Developed a rock-paper-scissors interaction system using RGB-D camera data to recognize hand gestures and generate responses from the HSR.

The applications were developed using ROS and Python.

## Demonstrations & Outreach

I participated in demonstrations of the RSH system at Fujita Health University Hospital for media representatives and international visitors.

- **Oct 28, 2019 — NHK Nagoya:** Participated in an RSH demonstration for NHK Nagoya's regional television program *Marutto!*.
- **Dec 5, 2019 — ASEAN Delegation Visit:** Participated in an RSH demonstration for international delegates attending the 17th ASEAN and Japan High Level Officials Meeting on Caring Societies.
