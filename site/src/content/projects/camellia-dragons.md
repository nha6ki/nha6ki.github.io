## Overview

From April 2016 to May 2017, I was a member of Camellia Dragons, Aichi Prefectural University's team competing in the [RoboCup Standard Platform League (SPL)](https://spl.robocup.org/).

The SPL was a robot soccer competition in which teams developed software for identical NAO humanoid robots to play soccer autonomously. My work focused on soccer strategy, player behavior, and goalkeeper motion, alongside public robotics demonstrations.

## Roles & Contributions

I was a member of the Behavior Group and served as the Strategy Group Lead.

My development responsibilities included:

- **Soccer Strategy:** Developing strategies for autonomous robot soccer.
- **Striker Behavior:** Developing the striker's role and associated behaviors.
- **Goalkeeper Motion:** Developing motion routines for the goalkeeper.

## Public Demonstrations & Outreach

In addition to RoboCup-related development, I participated in public events introducing NAO robots and robotics to wider audiences.

**Nov 5, 2016**

I developed a dancing program for a NAO humanoid robot as part of a robot show featuring projection mapping at Aichi Prefectural University's 70th-anniversary opening ceremony.
